02 · The investor we are building for

Indians don't pick a restaurant
without asking a friend.
The stakes here are higher.

Our conviction in advice isn't a value, it's a market fact. Despite years of discount-execution marketing from Groww and AngelOne, roughly 70% of MF assets still sit in Regular plans. People pay for guidance because they want the reassurance. That single fact earns the entire model.

Investor centricity · What the relationship owes them

They trust us with their money.
We owe them more than a screen.

  1. 01Protect first; never mis-sell.Do what is truly best for them. The protection posture is not just ethics, it is what lets the MFD relationship become a regulated advisory one later, with no discontinuity the investor can feel.
  2. 02Celebrate the redemption; teach the goal.A redemption that fulfils a goal is the defining win, not churn. Each fulfilled goal earns the next, and earns the investor's family as the next investors. Advocacy is the growth engine.
  3. 03They call us for the small things; we call them for the big.Inbound on demand for P2 / P3. Proactive outreach from us on P0 / P1. The service asymmetry is a rule, not a vibe.
  4. 04The more you hold, the calmer the screen.Information architecture for comfort, not overwhelm. More AUM means a quieter surface, detail one click away, the opposite of the industry's more-for-more reflex.

Establish trust, and AUM is the outcome, never the target.

The product posture · Foundational & horizontal

A consumer-internet product,
not a wealth-management one.

Reference points: Amazon, Flipkart, Groww, not the incumbent wealth apps. This is the posture every call below is made within, so it comes first.

The wealth-management default
  • The app exists to gather assets
  • Transaction first, craft second
  • Reliability is somebody else's problem
  • Service means raising a ticket
The way we build
  • Delight weighted equally with the transaction
  • Real discovery: filter, sort, compare, explain the why
  • Hard latency & uptime bars on money-moving flows
  • Goals embedded in the experience, not bolted on
Chosen against / the cost

Chosen against treating the app as a means to gather assets. The cost: a standing engineering-and-design investment most wealth firms never make, which is exactly what becomes the moat.

Call 01 · Posture

MFD now. RIA later.
On one stack.

V1 is a distributor business. The advisory line, fee-for-plan on Direct, comes as a fresh regulated build once the stack is unified and legacy is decommissioned. This is a bandwidth call before it is anything else: a clean regulated line cannot be built on a half-migrated stack with a team this size.

Step 1  Distribute · MFD, today→ Step 2  Unify · migrate & decommission→ Step 3  Advise · RIA, regulated
Chosen against / the cost

Chosen against phasing RIA in early, on a half-migrated stack. The cost: ~two years of an unused licence while Scripbox and ET Money sit in subscription advice. What de-risks the wait: the protection posture means we already run MFD to advisory ethical standards, so RIA, when it lands, formalises a relationship the investor has had all along. No discontinuity to cross.

FundsIndia08
Call 02 · Intelligence

One brain.
Three faces.

A single intelligence reasons over every investor's goals, holdings and life events, and expresses itself differently for the investor, the RM and the IFA. Same brain, three surfaces. This is the architectural expression of the unified, singular view of the investor.

One intelligence→ Investor surface· RM surface· IFA surface
Chosen against / the cost

Chosen against building separate intelligence per business line, the conventional structure, easier to ship line by line. The cost: one engine must serve three very different surfaces without forking, and persona-appropriate expression must be enforced centrally rather than per app. We carry the harder build to avoid three diverging brains later.

FundsIndia09
Call 03 · The investor experience

Goals are the index.
Not instruments.

Navigation is built around goals, not a list of funds. In V1 this is concrete and buildable: every scheme carries a risk score and a growth score derived from Alpha, Beta, Sharpe and the rest, then maps to the goal cohorts it suits. Goal creation (RM-enabled), the catalog's scheme intelligence, and the celebratory redemption all ship in V1.

Chosen against / the cost

Chosen against the industry's fund-first navigation. The cost: goal-led is a founding architectural choice, not a feature bolted on later, and it only pays off if we become world-class at getting people to set real goals.

Deliberately still open

Whether the RM book is valued on goals fulfilled is a real call with comp-plan consequences. Since V1 does not build for the RM, I am deferring it, not hand-waving it, to a deep deliberation in the Oct–Dec window.

  • Investor identity · one record, for life
  • The event spine · every change, remembered
  • The ledger · the line we never cross on money
  • The intelligence · the brain that learns
Buy · what doesn't
  • KYC

Chosen against / the cost

Chosen against building commodity rails for control, or buying an end-to-end platform for speed. The cost: a dependency on vendors who sit on the riskiest external clocks. We accept it so every unit of the team's attention goes to what makes us different. The line is drawn at differentiation, not at cost.

Call 05 · Scope

A narrow shelf, expanded on purpose.
Equity comes last, or not at all.

MF and SIF first, then FD and bonds, each phase proving on real customers before the next opens, on a schema that is multi-asset-ready from day one. Equity is not the next shelf item: it is derivatives, real-time settlement, an order-of-magnitude more scale, and elite incumbents. It is attempted with single-minded, well-funded focus, or not attempted at all.

MF · SIF→ FD · Bonds→ RIA→ Equity · its own discipline, last
Chosen against / the cost

Chosen against a broad multi-asset launch, or launching equity under-resourced to check a box. The cost: deferring a large, high-frequency, younger-investor segment for years. We would rather arrive properly than arrive early and lose to Zerodha, Groww and AngelOne.

Call 06 · Personas

Natural persons first.
Entities later.

V1 serves the personas that make up the mass-affluent base: resident individuals, minors operated by a guardian, and NRI / OCI investors. HUF and non-individual entities are deferred to the lines that need them, Private Wealth and B2B.

In V1  Resident individual· Minor, guardian-operated· NRI / OCI
Deferred  HUF· Entities & non-individuals
Chosen against / the cost

Chosen against carrying the full persona set, including HUF and entities, in V1. The cost: a meaningful slice of Indian wealth waits for a later phase. They are deferred by relevance to the V1 segment, not by difficulty, they arrive when the line that needs them does.

04 · What we will not build

What we refuse to build
says who we are.

We won't

Sell what doesn't protect

No crypto, no digital gold, no unregulated or speculative classes, whatever the demand or the trend. Only instruments that structurally protect the investor.

We won't

Oversell or mis-cross-sell

No overselling, and no cross-sell that isn't squarely in the customer's interest. Loan-against-MF is an honest, investor-initiated product, never a redemption-blocking retention trick.

We won't

Gamify investing

No streaks, no badges, no market-movement pings engineered for compulsion. It is not a game, and we will not make it one.

We won't

Use dark patterns

Redemption is as smooth as investment. We never trap money behind friction. The platform does not infantilise the investor.

06 · The team & how we build

Fifteen people, built to ship
what used to take forty.

Distributed-systems seniors paired with product engineers who think in entities and events, with AI tooling as a real multiplier, not a perk. A profile structured to deliver what needed thirty-to-forty engineers three years ago.

  1. 01Tech stays unified; Product carries the verticals.The single rule that keeps the stack singular. Vertical-level ownership and change originate in Product, never inside Tech.
  2. 02Insulated from BAU, reviewed on architecture.Not pulled into firefighting, not measured on quarterly metrics. Reviewed on architecture milestones, the single biggest defence against the build quietly becoming a second support team.
Chosen against / the cost

Chosen against a larger conventional team, or a BAU-integrated build. The cost: key-person concentration and lower raw throughput, accepted, because the insulation is what protects the horizon.
