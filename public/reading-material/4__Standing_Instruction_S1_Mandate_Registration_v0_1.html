What this is

J5 at build granularity. J5 is the governing view and stops deliberately at a live mandate, handing the debit cycle to the plan journey; this diagram draws the same territory with every gate that decides whether a mandate can be registered in the first place. No money moves anywhere on this page. Registration is the one-time act of setting an authority between the investor's bank account and the collecting party, and every later pull against that authority belongs to S3.

Drawing standard

Fixed across the series. Five of these are checked when the diagram is built, and the file is not written if any check fails.

  • Orthogonal only. Every connector segment is exactly horizontal or vertical. No diagonals. Checked.
  • Turns at the midpoint. Where a connector needs two turns, the crossing run sits at the exact midpoint of the span, so both legs are equal.
  • Edge attachment, separated. Connectors leave and enter only on a shape's edge, never at a corner and never inside it. Where several arrows meet the same shape they attach at different points along that edge rather than stacking on the midpoint, so each stays traceable. On a decision diamond that means a point along the relevant slanted face.
  • No connector crosses a shape. Checked.
  • Every arrowhead lands on a shape outline. Never in empty space. This matters most on a decision diamond, whose bounding box is not its outline, so a point that looks like the top edge floats clear of the shape. Checked.
  • No two arrowheads coincide. Same-colour heads must also stand clear of each other. Checked.
  • Phase bands occupy clear strips. A band is placed only in a horizontal strip containing no shape at all, so a band label can never collide with a box. Placement is computed, not eyeballed. Checked.
  • Colour carries meaning. Purple is the main path, amber is remediation, red is a blocking failure, teal is a shortcut or handoff.
  • Room over compactness. Height is unconstrained, so type stays large and nothing is crowded.

The flow

Where this sits in the Standing Instruction set

S1 Mandate registration, the core spine · S2 Persona and account variants · S3 The debit cycle · S4 Amendment, pause and termination · S5 Non-bank standing instructions. The block is named for standing instructions rather than mandates because only one of the three plan types has a mandate at all. A SIP is funded from a bank account and needs an authority over it. An SWP pays money outward and an STP moves value between schemes inside one fund house, so neither ever pulls from an account and neither has a mandate to register, amend or revoke. S5 exists to make that difference explicit rather than let it be assumed away.

The ceiling is not the instalment amount

This is the costliest setup error available on this page and it is worth stating on its own. The ceiling is the maximum per-debit value the mandate is registered for. Set it equal to the first instalment and the first step-up fails at the bank, and amending it needs a fresh authentication. Set it needlessly high and the investor has granted a standing authority far larger than they intended, which is a different kind of wrong and harder to argue was their choice. Headroom with a stated reason is the answer, and the amount of headroom is a product decision worth taking explicitly rather than picking a round number.

Grounded against, and what is drawn as a parameter

J5 for the spine, the acting parties and the Rejected terminal; the Order reference, section 6, for the mandate machinery; the Rails reference for eNACH, NACH and UPI Autopay at the level of which party acts. The register for D4 on the rail choice and the no-authentication line, D43 on the attachment contract, and D23 on the absence of cross-border UPI. Two thresholds appear as lines rather than numbers. The no-authentication ceiling moved more than once across 2025 and 2026, and D31 holds the per-bank effective limits open by design until Mandate PRD time. Those bank-level caps sit below the headline figures and are the ones that will bite, so the gate that checks them is drawn knowing it must exist without yet knowing its values.
