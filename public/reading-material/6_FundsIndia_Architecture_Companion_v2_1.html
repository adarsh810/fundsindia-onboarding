How to read this document

This is the drawn companion to the written canon, not a second authority. Where a diagram and a governing document disagree, the document wins and the diagram is corrected. Every diagram reads top-down: band header bars introduce each layer, component boxes sit inside bands, and the only arrows drawn connect adjacent bands. Synchronous rail semantics are stated on the rail band captions rather than drawn as long wires. Unsettled matter is marked where it sits, never drawn as settled, and the register's own distinction is preserved rather than collapsed. Red OPEN pills carry register items that are still open: D28 on Reconciliation and D47 and D48 on Servicing. Amber FOLLOW-UP pills carry decisions the register has closed but which have a named follow-up outstanding, so the shape is settled and only a parameter or a per-rail mechanic remains: D44 and D46 on Payments and Mandates, D8 on the Ledger, and D37 on Reconciliation. Amber ENG SESSION and INDICATIVE pills carry blocks awaiting the money-layer engineering session or drawn as strawman. A FOLLOW-UP pill is not licence to reopen the decision; it marks where a settled decision still needs a value. Sequence diagrams from v1.0 are removed by design: the journey layer (J1 to J13) owns flow sequencing, and this document stays at component and workflow altitude.

Diagram 1.1 · L0 platform overview

The whole platform on one page: investor surfaces over a stateless read layer, eleven bounded contexts that own state and publish to the typed bitemporal event spine, the off-spine projections including the ledger as a reconciliation projection of RTA truth, every external dependency behind a vendor-neutral rail ACL, the migration bridge as a post-launch horizontal, and the seams deliberately held open for V1.5 and V2. Two constraints govern everything drawn here: no investor money ever transits the platform (no-pooling, satisfied by construction through the aggregator route per D1), and the intelligence substrate runs capture-on, internal-read-on, with investor-facing read and any actioning off until V2.

Open decision · carried from Technical Architecture View v0.3

Recommendation-surface policing. The three RECO-tagged surfaces (Goal Engine, Portfolio Service overlay, Discovery pills) can each read as recommendation-shaped. Open: do they police the MFD dissection-not-prescription boundary independently, or route through one shared enforcement chokepoint? Structural decision, not copy. Wording is a separate Compliance-in-the-loop exercise and stays parked. Unresolved as of this draft; drawn as three independent pills pending the ruling.

Diagram 2.1 · Identity, KYC & Consent

Onboarding is one resumable state machine over the investor's journey, with persona shape (Resident, NRI, OCI, Minor; Joint as an overlay) applied as overlays rather than separate flows. Verification is multi-modal behind the Identity Rail, with the canonical KYC status ladder (Registered, Validated, On Hold) owned by the KYC state machine, not by any vendor's vocabulary. The four-way entity split is drawn explicitly because it is the most-tempting thing to collapse: Investor, Identity, Commercial Relationship, and Relationship Graph stay separate entities. Consent and Authorization is a legally operative home in its own right: purpose-bound, version-tracked, non-repudiable artifacts covering DPDP consent, AA consent, and mandate authorisation. One operational truth shapes the refresh design: KRAs push nothing, so downgrade detection is by poll. The AML screening band (sanctions, PEP, adverse media, source of funds) is drawn at the old Companion's depth and held pending Compliance confirmation of V1 scope.

Diagram 3.1 · Catalog, Scheme Intelligence & Discovery

The Catalog holds scheme facts as first-class, versioned attributes, and every flow reads the attribute rather than assuming from the category: tax buckets derive from the fund-house attribute and never from a labelled default (D18), NRI and OCI eligibility including US and Canada handling is per scheme (D32, D20), and each SIF strategy's cut-offs, redemption windows, and settlement mechanics are captured per strategy (D14). Scheme Intelligence stays factual and derived. Discovery owns no state and every ranked surface runs through the governed console. The MFD-not-RIA framing is a build constraint here, not a copy note: suitability-based merchandising, never advice for a fee, written into the Catalog PRD. SIF stays technically available while launch merchandising is held for the V2 RM layer.

Diagram 4.1 · Goal Engine & Portfolio Dissection

Goal is a first-class, mandatory domain entity with its own lifecycle; orders and plans attach to goals, and progress is derived, never judged. The Portfolio Service is dissection-only in V1: it states composition and concentration as facts and never prescribes, the discipline being the difference between 'stock is 8% of equity' and 'too much weightage'. The Financial Picture (J13) brings external holdings in through the AA rail under purpose-bound, revocable consent, displaying gross-only returns clearly qualified (D49). It carries the one go-live gate on this diagram: Sahamati / FIU certification must land by March 2027, and this feature is the cleanly-cuttable element if it slips.

Diagram 5.1 · Order & Execution

Every money instruction is an order through one lifecycle (J6), whether typed by the investor or generated by a plan. The pre-check gate reads reflected external state and trusts it inside the agreed freshness window (D37); the rare post-acceptance rejection is a defined edge. The state machine names are canon: OrderPlaced is the kept name, OrderSubmitted is retired, and the sequence runs to OrderSettled or OrderFailed under forward pricing and the funds-realisation rule. A confirmed holding shows only once payment has cleared (D36), and the bounce-after-provisional-allotment unwind is a compensating correction whose per-rail mechanics are confirmed at each rail's integration (D8, flagged on the Ledger). FP carries V1; BSE StAR MF joins at V1.5 behind the same vendor-neutral port.

Diagram 6.1 · Payments & Mandates

The money path satisfies no-pooling by construction: collection runs from the investor's bank through Razorpay's validated-payment and routing products to the fund house, and redemption money travels from the scheme's account directly to the investor's registered bank; no account of ours is ever in the path (D1, verbal confirmation in hand, written confirmation assumed incoming). The journey boundary is fixed: paying immediately belongs to Order, anything registered now to debit later belongs here (D44). Mandates are UMRN-bound, orders attach at authenticated and unwind on rejection (D43), and one mandate can back several SIPs within its ceiling. The plans engine runs the D9 failure machinery: a failed instalment fails alone, three consecutive failures pause the plan, and the KYC pause (D46) is a distinct state with distinct messaging that is never dressed as a payment failure.

Follow-ups on this diagram · D44 and D46

D44 ceiling-breach rule: when a new SIP would push combined debits past the mandate ceiling on a shared date, block-at-setup versus allow-and-fail; the leaning is block-at-setup since the alternative knowingly feeds the D9 failure counter. D46 pause-stacking rule: a plan can plausibly hold both pauses at once; resuming requires both to clear and instalment generation gates while either is active. Both settle in the pending HoE engineering session and land in the Mandate and Plans PRDs. Per your ruling, D31 per-bank caps and the D9 threshold parameter are held for the Mandate and Plans PRDs and are not flagged here.

Diagram 7.1 · Folio

The RTA is the system of record and the platform holds a reflected copy, consolidated to one view per PAN. Folio reuse follows the four-way matching key: same investor, same fund house, same holding mode, same repatriability class; all four match means reuse, any one differs means a new folio is correct and intentional (D42). A folio is born inside the first purchase (D38); the discrete creation path exists only as an internal capability for transmission and half-success recovery, and no create-folio button exists anywhere in V1. Holdings are SOA-only with no demat fork (D3), NRE and NRO money never share a folio (D27), the first holder's status governs mixed-residency folios (D30), and nomination follows the 29 May 2026 circular rules (D10).

Diagram 8.1 · Ledger

The ledger is a double-entry, bitemporal, immutable projection of RTA truth; on any conflict the RTA wins and the ledger restates. Because no investor money is ever held, no cash custody accounts exist. The doctrine band and the consumers band are settled canon. Everything between them, the posting pipeline, the account taxonomy, the posting patterns, and the temporal policy, is drawn as a deliberate strawman at full detail to give the HoE money-layer engineering session something concrete to argue with: it is faster to correct a drawn wrong answer than to specify from a blank page. Every unsettled block carries a pill, OPEN where the register item is open and FOLLOW-UP where the register has closed the decision and only a residual remains, and the callout strip at the top of the diagram states the status plainly. The settled anchors hold regardless of how the session lands: postings stay at investor and folio altitude with household as a reporting projection only, corrections compensate and never delete, and NPS unit and bond face-value accounts stay out of V1.

Indicative · pending the HoE money-layer engineering session

Status. Bands B to E are a strawman, not a specification. The deepest gap on this diagram is not a register item: posting semantics, the account taxonomy, and the event-to-posting mapping are unwritten, and the flag points at the engineering session by name so it is not mistaken for something the register can resolve. The drawn detail exists to anchor that session, and every block carrying an OPEN pill is expected to change shape there.

What the session must settle, in the order the strawman raises it

1. Account taxonomy. Confirm or replace the minimal set drawn: settled units per investor-folio-scheme, a money-in-flight mirror, charges and withholding legs, payout receivable. The sharpest sub-question: are provisional units a separate account or a state on the units account? The answer decides how the D8 unwind posts. 2. Event-to-posting mapping. Which spine events post and which are informational; the idempotency key per posting; and ordering tolerance, which matters concretely because the FP assessment found webhooks in beta with no ordering guarantee and no replay, so the mapper must be order-tolerant with reconciliation as the corrector. 3. Money-in-flight under no-pooling. The legs are mirrors of money moving between the investor bank, the aggregator, and the fund house; they must be named so they can never be read as custody. 4. D8 unwind per rail. What the FP bounce-after-allotment flow emits and the exact compensating pattern; repeats as a named use case at BSE StAR integration. 5. Restatement policy. What triggers a valid-time restatement versus a compensating correction, and the statement-reconstruction guarantee that must survive both. 6. Precision and batching. Unit decimal conventions per RTA and whether multi-scheme batch confirmations post as one transaction or several.

Diagram 9.1 · Reconciliation

We are not the system of record, so reconciliation is where truth is re-established: a three-way match of platform ledger against RTA feeds against payment-rail confirmations, with an exception workflow that treats post-acceptance rejection as a defined edge. Data flows are designed to the RTA and depository feed cycles, largely daily and batch, with buffers baked in; reflected copies of KYC, folio, and encumbrance state are trusted inside the agreed window. During migration the same engine runs three books per cohort as the cutover gate.

Follow-up and open on this diagram · D37 and D28

D37 freshness windows: the direction is settled (design to feed cycles), but the concrete windows and reconciliation cadence are confirmed in the engineering session with the feed providers; the cadence values on this diagram are drawn as design intent, not confirmed numbers. D28 encumbrance feed: the check exists in V1; the lien-versus-pledge feed mechanics are deliberately parked until the lending phase and the seam is drawn accordingly.

Diagram 10.1 · Relationships, Referral & Servicing

The relationship substrate is a soft graph in V1 (guardian-minor, joint holders, referral edges) with the rich household and referral-depth graph held as a V2 seam behind the same entity. The Relationship Graph is its own entity in the four-way split and is never conflated with Investor, Identity, or Commercial Relationship; RM and IFA workflows stay in the legacy CRM in V1 via the outbound ingest edge. Servicing owns the lifecycle events that arrive after onboarding, and it carries the two truly open register items: how far the platform builds for residency change (D47) and for heir claims beyond the drawn nominee path (D48). Both are build-extent choices with relationship consequences, and neither has a recorded ruling.

Open on this diagram · D47 and D48

D47 residency change: a resident becoming an NRI (or returning) moves KYC, bank account class, folio repatriability tagging, and tax withholding together; the serving build extent is unrecorded. D48 heir claims: the nominee path is drawn; the no-nominee tiered-documentation path and the contested claim need a servicing design whose extent (full tracking and guidance versus minimal surface) is an open ruling. The recommended position in the register is to build the tracking and guidance.

Diagram 11.1 · Workflow · onboard to first settled order (J7)

The spine journey drawn at architecture altitude: which component owns each step, read left to right within a phase and top to bottom across phases, with the owning context named on every box. The KYC Validated gate (D39) sits between onboarding and the first purchase; the goal attaches before the order because Goal is mandatory, not decorative; the first order carries the folio-create instruction so the folio is born inside the purchase (D38); and the money moves investor bank to fund house through the aggregator with no account of ours in the path. The half-success edge, folio created but payment failed, is handled by the internal recovery path and the compensating unwind, stated on the flag strip rather than drawn as a branch.

Diagram 11.2 · Workflow · SIP instalment, honoured and dishonoured (J8)

One instalment, both endings. The honoured phase is deliberately boring: debit, aggregator route, allot, settle, counter resets. The dishonoured phase is the D9 machinery in motion: the instalment fails alone and is never re-presented in a way that could invest twice for one month, the consecutive-failure counter advances, three consecutive failures pause the plan with its own honest message, and resumption follows investor action or a successful debit. The KYC pause (D46) is a distinct state with distinct messaging; whether the two pauses stack, and how resumption gates when both are active, is the flagged open item for the engineering session.

Diagram 11.3 · Workflow · redemption (J9)

Units out, money home, and the payout never touches the platform: proceeds travel from the scheme account directly to the investor's registered bank. Pre-checks read dormancy and run the encumbrance check before acceptance; pricing is forward NAV by cut-off from Catalog attributes. For NRI folios the RTA computes the withholding and the platform reflects the net rather than computing tax itself (D45), which is also exactly what the investor sees at the end: the net amount, stated plainly.

Diagram 11.4 · Workflow · the daily reconciliation cycle

The cycle that re-establishes truth every day. Feeds land from the RTAs and the payment rail, reflected state refreshes by poll, and the three-way match settles what agrees while routing what disagrees to an owned, aged exception queue. Post-acceptance rejection is a defined edge with defined handling, and corrections post compensating entries with the original line surviving. The one open item is the cadence itself: windows are designed to feed cycles with buffers, and the concrete values await the engineering session with the feed providers (D37). During migration the same engine runs three books per cohort as the cutover gate.

Diagram 11.5 · Workflow · financial picture pull (J13)

External holdings arrive only under the investor's own purpose-bound, revocable consent, fetched through the vendor-neutral AA port so the multi-AA gateway drops in at V1.5 without touching the domain. What comes back is captured and internally readable for validation, and nothing more: the intelligence boundary holds. The presented view is gross-only and clearly qualified (D49), the dissection stance applies to external holdings exactly as it does to on-platform ones, and revocation stops fetches and purges per DPDP. The flag strip carries the one hard dependency: Sahamati / FIU certification must land by go-live, and this workflow is the cleanly-cuttable element if it slips.

Diagram 12.1 · Event spine & data foundation

The spine in its own right: typed and schema-versioned against a registry, bitemporally stamped, immutable and fully replayable. Four event families ride it (financial, lifecycle, relationship, operational) and membership is decided at design time. The discipline runs both ways: publication is reliable so no state change is lost before the spine, and consumers are idempotent and order-tolerant, which is not optional given the FP finding that external webhooks arrive in beta with no ordering guarantee and no replay. The behavioural clickstream rides a permanently separate plane, Martech reads that plane and not the spine, and the only lawful join between the two happens in the lake under consent and purpose binding.

Diagram 13.1 · Intelligence seam · the four-flow boundary

The V1 intelligence posture stated as four switches: capture-write on, internal read on, investor-facing read off, action and write-back off, all set until V2. Capture is built now so V2 starts with history rather than a cold start, and every captured signal carries its purpose and its consent. Internal read is a validation lens for data quality and aggregate analytics with a hard edge: no investor surface, no RM tool, and no ranking reads the substrate in V1. What stays off is held as three named, dashed seams so turning them on is a bolt-on, not a rebuild.

Diagram 14.1 · Security, PII, DPDP & consent planes

Five planes that wrap everything else drawn in this document. Zero trust at the perimeter and between services; a PII plane where personal data is tokenised at rest and every read is purpose-bound; a consent plane where the ledger is legally operative and withdrawal is honoured end to end across every store; an audit posture where every read and write is attributable and ARN / EUIN attribution rides every order; and residency and continuity, with India cloud residency, multi-AZ failover, and stated RPO and RTO targets. The CSCRF posture and the DevSecOps pipeline are drawn as part of compliance rather than as an engineering nicety, because for a SEBI-regulated intermediary that is what they are.

Diagram 15.1 · ACL discipline & rail map with V1.5 seams

The rule is stated before the map: one adapter per domain even when a single vendor spans two, the domain interface written first with the vendor mapping to it, and vendor SDKs never entering business logic. The V1 map has four adapters, each fronting a system of record that is not us. The FP posture band pre-loads the six documentation-pass findings that shape consumer-side design and the live 63-question engagement: beta webhooks, certification gaps, NRI and OCI coverage asymmetries, zero SIF documentation, pull-only KYC downgrade eventing, and NEFT / RTGS absent from documented modes. Both V1.5 seams drop in behind existing ports, and the Finarkein-versus-Finvu distinction is stated on the seam itself: an orchestrator is a different stack layer from an AA, not a substitute.

Diagram 16.1 · Migration bridge & legacy substrate

The strategy in one line: migrate the experience layer, not the mandates. NACH mandates bind to UMRN, which is the utility code plus bank account plus sponsor bank; PAN and ARN are not parties to the mandate contract, so the portability hypothesis does not hold and the book stays legally undisturbed while the experience moves. The substrate is read-only against legacy, translates identities PAN-anchored into the four-way split, moves 1,000 to 2,000 investors a day across roughly six months, opening once the V1.5 tooling is built and proven rather than on a date, which on the current sequence is roughly July 2027, and gates every cohort on the dual-run reconciliation. The dependencies band carries the decision inputs: the BSE StAR slippage coupling (window closed, watched), the signing-key and brand decisions, and the Compliance ruling on pre-authenticated deep links. One box carries a pill of its own kind, because one fact decides the whole mandate story: who holds the legacy mandate book's utility code.

Pivotal unknown · the utility-code holder

Whether the legacy mandate book’s NPCI utility code sits with BSE / ICCL via XSIP, with FundsIndia’s own code, or with a third-party gateway determines the entire mandate migration path: it decides whether debits continue undisturbed, whether the collecting-entity preservation at SIP touch events is even needed, and how large the per-user fresh-mandate fallback population is. This is a fact to be identified, not a decision to be taken, and it is the single highest-leverage retrieval in this section. Everything else in 16.1 is drawn to remain valid under any answer.
