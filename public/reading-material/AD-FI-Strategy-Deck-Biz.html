Positioning · What FundsIndia is

Relationship quality is the foundation.

AUM and transactions are outcomes.

  1. 01 **We hold a household's financial story across decades.**Not a transaction, not an account: the financial life of a household, carried across generations. The horizon is the lifetime and beyond it.
  2. 02 **Our incentives align with the investor's growth and protection.**We win when they grow and stay protected. The moment our interest and theirs diverge, the franchise is already lost.
  3. 03 **One unified stack, not three verticalised ones.**B2C, B2B and Private Wealth run on a common backend with a single intelligence; the surfaces are the tip of the iceberg. Vertical ownership lives in Product, never in Tech.
  4. 04 **One Investor ID across all three businesses.**A single investor may hold a B2C RM, a B2B IFA and a Wealth RM at once, all hanging off one identity. One person, one record, many relationships.

One platform, three front doors: B2C, B2B and Private Wealth, each with a defined place and sequence. No line is an afterthought.

02 · The investor we are building for

Indians don't pick a restaurant

without asking a friend. The stakes here are higher.

Our conviction in advice isn't a value, it's a market fact. Despite years of discount-execution marketing from Groww and AngelOne, roughly 70% of MF assets still sit in Regular plans. People pay for guidance because they want the reassurance. That single fact earns the entire model.

SEBI Investor Survey, Jan 2026 ~70–30 Regular vs Direct, by assets

****FundsIndia 04

Investor centricity · What the relationship owes them

They trust us with their money.

We owe them more than a screen.

  1. 01 **Protect first; never mis-sell.**Do what is truly best for them. The protection posture is not just ethics, it is what lets the MFD relationship become a regulated advisory one later, with no discontinuity the investor can feel.
  2. 02 **Celebrate the redemption; teach the goal.**A redemption that fulfils a goal is the defining win, not churn. Each fulfilled goal earns the next, and earns the investor's family as the next investors. Advocacy is the growth engine.
  3. 03 **They call us for the small things; we call them for the big.**Inbound on demand for P2 / P3. Proactive outreach from us on P0 / P1. The service asymmetry is a rule, not a vibe.
  4. 04 **The more you hold, the calmer the screen.**Information architecture for comfort, not overwhelm. More AUM means a quieter surface, detail one click away, the opposite of the industry's more-for-more reflex.

Establish trust, and AUM is the outcome, never the target.

The product posture · Foundational & horizontal

A consumer-internet product,

not a wealth-management one.

Reference points: Zerodha, Myntra, Groww, not the incumbent wealth apps. This is the posture every call below is made within, so it comes first.

The wealth-management default

  • The app exists to gather assets
  • Transaction first, craft second
  • Reliability is somebody else's problem
  • Service means raising a ticket

The way we build

  • Delight weighted equally with the transaction
  • Real discovery: filter, sort, compare, explain the why
  • Hard latency & uptime bars on money-moving flows
  • Goals embedded in the experience, not bolted on

Chosen against / the cost

Chosen against treating the app as a means to gather assets. The cost: a standing engineering-and-design investment most wealth firms never make, which is exactly what becomes the moat.

03 · The calls behind the build

Strategy is what you

say no to.

Six calls that shaped the platform. Each is named with the road we did not take and the price we chose to pay, because a decision you can't price isn't a decision, it's a hope.

1 MFD → RIA, one stack / 2 One brain, not three / 3 Goals at the centre / 4 Buy the rails, build the brain / 5 A narrow shelf, on purpose / 6 Natural persons first

****FundsIndia 07

1

Call 01 · Posture

MFD now. RIA later.

On one stack.

V1 is a distributor business. The advisory line, fee-for-plan on Direct, comes as a fresh regulated build once the stack is unified and legacy is decommissioned. This is a bandwidth call before it is anything else: a clean regulated line cannot be built on a half-migrated stack with a team this size.

Step 1  Distribute · MFD, today → Step 2  Unify · migrate & decommission → Step 3  Advise · RIA, regulated

Chosen against / the cost

Chosen against phasing RIA in early, on a half-migrated stack. The cost: ~two years of an unused licence while Scripbox and ET Money sit in subscription advice. What de-risks the wait: the protection posture means we already run MFD to advisory ethical standards, so RIA, when it lands, formalises a relationship the investor has had all along. No discontinuity to cross.

****FundsIndia 08

2

Call 02 · Intelligence

One brain.

Three faces.

A single intelligence reasons over every investor's goals, holdings and life events, and expresses itself differently for the investor, the RM and the IFA. Same brain, three surfaces. This is the architectural expression of the unified, singular view of the investor.

One intelligence → Investor surface · RM surface · IFA surface

Chosen against / the cost

Chosen against building separate intelligence per business line, the conventional structure, easier to ship line by line. The cost: one engine must serve three very different surfaces without forking, and persona-appropriate expression must be enforced centrally rather than per app. We carry the harder build to avoid three diverging brains later.

****FundsIndia 09

3

Call 03 · The investor experience

Goals are the index.

Not instruments.

Navigation is built around goals, not a list of funds. In V1 this is concrete and buildable: every scheme carries a risk score and a growth score derived from Alpha, Beta, Sharpe and the rest, then maps to the goal cohorts it suits. Goal creation (RM-enabled), the catalog's scheme intelligence, and the celebratory redemption all ship in V1.

Chosen against / the cost

Chosen against the industry's fund-first navigation. The cost: goal-led is a founding architectural choice, not a feature bolted on later, and it only pays off if we become world-class at getting people to set real goals.

Deliberately still open

Whether the RM book is valued on goals fulfilled is a real call with comp-plan consequences. Since V1 does not build for the RM, I am deferring it, not hand-waving it, to a deep deliberation in the Oct–Dec window.

Buy what's commodity.

Build what compounds.

Build · what compounds

  • The event spine · every change, remembered
  • The ledger · the line we never cross on money
  • The intelligence · the brain that learns

Buy · what doesn't

  • KYC
  • Payments
  • RTA integration

Chosen against / the cost

Chosen against building commodity rails for control, or buying an end-to-end platform for speed. The cost: a dependency on vendors who sit on the riskiest external clocks. We accept it so every unit of the team's attention goes to what makes us different. The line is drawn at differentiation, not at cost.

Call 05 · Scope

A narrow shelf, expanded on purpose.

Equity comes last, or not at all.

MF and SIF first, then FD and bonds, each phase proving on real customers before the next opens, on a schema that is multi-asset-ready from day one. Equity is not the next shelf item: it is derivatives, real-time settlement, an order-of-magnitude more scale, and elite incumbents. It is attempted with single-minded, well-funded focus, or not attempted at all.

MF · SIF → FD · Bonds → RIA → Equity · its own discipline, last

The trade-off / where I want your read

A staged shelf rather than a broad multi-asset launch, and no under-resourced equity to check a box. The cost: deferring a large, high-frequency, younger-investor segment for years. Owned together: how fast the shelf widens into PMS, AIF, Unlisted and NCDs is a PW call as much as a platform one. The schema is ready from day one, so sequencing is a business conversation, not a rebuild.

Call 06 · Personas

Natural persons first.

Entities, with the line that owns them.

V1 serves the personas that make up the mass-affluent base: resident individuals, minors operated by a guardian, and NRI / OCI investors. HUF and non-individual entities land with Private Wealth and B2B: not as an afterthought, but designed with those teams, so the entity model is right for the book it will carry.

In V1  Resident individual · Minor, guardian-operated · NRI / OCI

With PW & B2B  HUF · Entities & non-individuals

The trade-off / where I want your read

Sequencing the full persona set behind the V1 mass-affluent base. The cost: a meaningful slice of Indian wealth waits for a later phase. The open question for PW & B2B: the timing is set; the model is not. The entity and HUF design should be shaped by the teams who live with those relationships, an invitation to build it together, not a decision handed down.

04 · What we will not build

What we refuse to build

says who we are.

We won't

Sell what doesn't protect

No crypto, no digital gold, no unregulated or speculative classes, whatever the demand or the trend. Only instruments that structurally protect the investor.

We won't

Oversell or mis-cross-sell

No overselling, and no cross-sell that isn't squarely in the customer's interest. Loan-against-MF is an honest, investor-initiated product, never a redemption-blocking retention trick.

We won't

Gamify investing

No streaks, no badges, no market-movement pings engineered for compulsion. It is not a game, and we will not make it one.

We won't

Use dark patterns

Redemption is as smooth as investment. We never trap money behind friction. The platform does not infantilise the investor.

Migration · The existing book

Zero disruption, by design.

The existing book of ~200,000 investors is untouched, by construction rather than by promise. Four principles make it so.

  1. 01 **Net-new first; the book waits for proof.**V1 launches to new investors only. Migration opens around July 2027, after a stability window on the live platform.
  2. 02 **Paced, cohort by cohort. Never big-bang.**Roughly 2,000 accounts a day, each cohort verified before the next moves; the full book crosses in about six months.
  3. 03 **SIPs and mandates keep running.**The experience migrates; the money rails underneath do not break. A fresh mandate is a per-investor fallback, never a cohort-level demand.
  4. 04 **Nobody is stranded, nothing is forced.**Every investor lands on the unified platform by V3 with folios, history and relationships intact. Legacy retires only when the last account has crossed.

Disruption is not a risk we are managing. It is a case we have designed out.

The plan · B2B & Private Wealth in V2

Later by sequence, not by priority.

V1 proves the substrate on the simplest full test: mass-affluent, self-serve, natural persons. B2B and Private Wealth then land on a platform already hardened on live money, and the shape of that landing is something I want to plan with each of you, starting now.

B2B · The IFA line

  • RM workstation first: every servicing flow proven on our own RMs
  • IFA console follows on that engine: same brain, IFA face
  • Shared design layer starts in the V1.5 window
  • The wait buys the IFA a proven platform, not an experiment

Private Wealth

  • HUF & entity personas land with the PW build
  • The shelf widens with it: PMS · AIF · Unlisted · NCDs
  • White-glove, RM-assisted migration for the Wealth book
  • One Investor ID: the full household picture, from day one

The trade-off / what I'm asking of you

One substrate proven first, rather than three lines' surfaces stood up in parallel. The cost: B2B and Private Wealth land in the V2 band. What I need from PW & B2B now: your requirements shape V2 from the V1.5 window, so when your line lands, it is designed with your book in mind, not retrofitted to it.
