Section 03

Table Stakes

The baseline capability every B2C wealth platform must have. Having these does not win — failing to have them disqualifies. Execution quality across these dimensions varies dramatically between players.

01

Paperless KYC & Onboarding

Aadhaar eKYC, PAN verification, BSE/MF-registered in minutes. Differentiator: speed. Groww does it in under 3 minutes; legacy bank platforms still require branch visits for edge cases.

02

Direct MF Access

Zero-commission direct plans across all AMFI-registered AMCs. Now table stakes since 2013 SEBI regulation — but regular vs direct model remains a fault line between platforms.

03

SIP & Lump-sum Flows

UPI / eMandate / net banking integration. Step-up SIP, pause, modify, skip. The mechanics must be invisible — any friction in SIP processing is fatal to retention.

04

Portfolio Dashboard

XIRR, absolute returns, fund-level breakdown, gain/loss tracking. Must show the complete picture in one scroll. Insufficient dashboards lose mass-affluent users to aggregator tools.

05

Fund Discovery & Filters

Category, AMC, rating, returns filters; fund comparison tools. The filter quality determines whether the platform serves beginners or informed investors — or both.

06

Tax Reporting

Capital gains statements, ELSS / 80C tracking, ITR-ready reports. LTCG / STCG accuracy is critical — errors here destroy trust permanently.

07

Notifications & Nudges

SIP reminders, NAV alerts, market news, goal milestone alerts. The quality of nudges — contextual vs generic — is where differentiation begins. Generic push = ignored.

08

Mobile-first Experience

iOS + Android apps with fast load, biometric login, UPI deep-link. App store ratings above 4.2 are now baseline expectations. Sub-3s load time on 4G is non-negotiable.

The table stakes trap Every player has all eight. The real battleground is what comes after the SIP — who can move the investor from a transactional relationship to a wealth-building relationship. Platforms that stop at table stakes will be commoditised. The winning move is owning the answer to: "Now what?" Section 04

FundsIndia Strategy Overview

A coherent strategy for FundsIndia to move from Niche Player to Leaders quadrant in 18 months, anchored by two newly validated hidden assets: a 15-year research alpha track record and the chief engineer's full transition to the new stack build.

4%

Alpha over Nifty — for 15 consecutive years

FundsIndia's research team has delivered 4% alpha over the Nifty benchmark consistently — through the 2008 GFC, 2013 taper tantrum, 2020 COVID crash, 2022 rate spike, and every election cycle in between. This is a multi-regime validated track record that most PMS managers charge 2%+ in fees and fail to match. Almost no one outside FundsIndia's existing user base knows this exists.

Note: Track record to be formally audited by a Big 4 firm for SEBI-compliant public disclosure before marketing use.

Top four moats — research alpha, data depth, engineering knowledge, loyal AUM — are non-purchasable with capital. No competitor can buy a 15-year track record.

Strategic Pillars

01

Own the Mass-Affluent Gap

Groww serves the beginner (₹0–₹10L portfolio). Dezerv/Waterfield serve the HNI (₹1Cr+). Nobody credibly serves the ₹50L–₹5Cr mass-affluent investor with real advisory. This is 8–10M households in India. FundsIndia should own this segment entirely.

02

Advisory-First, Instrument-Agnostic

The product question must always be: "what's the right instrument for this money, for this goal, at this tax bracket?" — not "here are 2,000 mutual funds, pick one." MF, bonds, NPS, FD, SGB — all instruments under one intelligent recommendation layer.

03

Fee-Based, Conflict-Free Revenue

Subscription fee (₹4,999–₹9,999/yr) for research-backed advisory + trail on regular plans as secondary revenue. Three streams create resilience. SEBI's direction toward fee transparency means early movers benefit from regulatory tailwind.

04

Make the Alpha Visible

Audit and publish the 15-year track record. Build the onboarding hook around the alpha gap: "Here's what your current portfolio returned. Here's what FundsIndia Select returned over the same period. The gap is ₹X." Let the number do the selling.

05

Data + Trust Flywheel

15 years of data → better recommendations → more trust → users consolidate more assets → more data. This flywheel is unbeatable for any new entrant including Jio BlackRock. The key is making the data intelligence visible in the product so users feel the advantage.

06

WestBridge as Zero-CAC Channel

WestBridge's portfolio companies (Freshworks, Juspay, Springworks) are software companies with well-paid employees who are exactly FundsIndia's target user. One B2B deal = thousands of high-value users at zero acquisition cost.

North star — the one-sentence strategy

"FundsIndia is the advisory-first, multi-instrument wealth platform for India's mass-affluent investor — the smartest thing you can do with money you've already earned."

Section 05

18-Month Phase Sequence

Five sequential phases beginning with a pre-start period (now to September 2025) that is new to the revised plan — enabled by the research alpha and chief engineer assets. Tech build starts September 2025 with a two-team architecture: greenfield team (Flipkart-calibre engineers) builds new stack; legacy team does KTLO with enhancements.

0

Pre-start — Now to September 2025

New phase — did not exist in original map. Enabled by research asset and chief engineer. Two things that cannot wait: auditing the track record and writing the book of known failures.

Immediate · Critical path

Commission the track record audit

Engage a Big 4 firm to formally verify the 15-year alpha — methodology, benchmark comparison, survivorship bias treatment, SEBI-compliant format. Takes 6–10 weeks. Starting now means the certificate is ready when the new platform launches in March 2026. Starting in September means it's not ready until mid-2026.

★ New — critical path

Immediate · De-risks timeline

Chief engineer writes the Book of Known Failures

Before writing a single line of new code, the chief engineer documents every production failure, AMC quirk, CAMS reconciliation edge case, BSE StarMF format deviation, NRI folio corner case, and payment gateway timeout across 10 years. This is the reliability design brief for the new team — they build knowing what must never break.

★ New — de-risks entire timeline

Positioning · Strategic

Lock the ICP before September

Ideal Customer Profile must be decided and agreed across growth, product, and tech before September. Recommended: ₹25L–₹5Cr portfolio investor who already invests but is underserved on advice quality. Not a beginner. Not an HNI. The mass-affluent investor who reads Mint and is quietly dissatisfied with DIY investing.

★ New — anchors everything

Growth · Audience building

Build the waitlist before the product launches

Growth team's job before September: start building an audience for the alpha reveal. A newsletter, waitlist page, early content on debt investing and portfolio construction. Goal: 10,000–20,000 engaged, right-profile subscribers ready to convert the moment the audited track record goes live.

★ New — demand before product

⚠ If the audit is not commissioned now, the alpha track record cannot be used in marketing until mid-2026. That is a 9-month delay on the most powerful go-to-market asset in the business. Highest-priority action in the entire plan.
