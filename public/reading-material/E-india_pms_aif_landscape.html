India PMS & AIF service-provider landscape

The custody-and-administration stack behind Portfolio Management Services and Alternative Investment Funds. Unlike the retail rails (exchange-routed, low-ticket), these are private, HNI and institutional structures: the investor's assets sit with a custodian, a trustee oversees the pool for an AIF, and a fund administrator keeps the books. Read top-to-bottom; PMS and AIF share most of the lower layers and differ mainly in legal structure. Names verified against provider sites and SEBI/APMI sources; figures like ticket sizes and tax treatment shift, so treat those as orientation rather than advice.
