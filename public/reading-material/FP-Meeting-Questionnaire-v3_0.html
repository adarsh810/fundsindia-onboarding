How to use this

Each question carries a priority. P0 means we cannot launch on FP without a good answer, and where a specific answer would stop the deal, we say so plainly in the question. P1 means a weak answer costs us extra build or an accepted risk, not the deal. P2 shapes the roadmap conversation and blocks nothing.

Where we ask for something in writing, a verbal yes in the room does not close the question. That is not distrust; it is what our Compliance function will ask us for afterwards, and we would rather collect it once.

Context you should have. We are rebuilding FundsIndia's platform for a March 2027 launch. We operate as a distributor (MFD), not an adviser. We serve resident, NRI, OCI, and minor investors from day one. We already have around two hundred thousand investors whose book migrates after launch. Mutual funds and SIF are both first-class at V1. Our platform reflects external truth and is never the system of record, which is why so many of these questions are about how faithfully we can see what you see.

First, what we think we already know

Please correct anything here that is wrong. A correction is more useful to us than an answer to any question below.

Our reading of your documentation

  • Order states. Purchases, redemptions, and switches share one state machine: pending, confirmed, submitted, cancelled, successful, failed, reversed (plus under-review on ONDC). Rejections carry machine-readable failure codes. We are building our order spine against exactly this.
  • Reversal is a real state. A previously successful order can be reversed and the units come back off. This matters to us more than you might expect, and we will ask about the procedure behind it.
  • Mandate states. Created, received (BSE), submitted, approved, rejected, cancelled. We read "submitted" as the investor having authorised and the bank not yet having accepted. We are attaching orders to mandates on that reading.
  • Mandate rails and limits. eNACH and UPI Autopay, both live via Razorpay and BillDesk. eNACH to one crore, UPI Autopay to one lakh. Above one lakh, the answer is eNACH rather than a per-debit approval flow.
  • Failure codes are granular. A bank's internal debit cap being hit reads differently from an insufficient balance. Data problems on a first order read differently from payment problems. This was one of our bigger worries and your taxonomy answers it.
  • The full transaction matrix exists. SIP, SWP, STP, switch, partial and full redemption, plus pause, skip, modify, and cancel on plans. STP is a switch plan with no mandate, which is correct: the buy leg funds itself from the sell leg.
  • Distributor stamping. Partners carry the ARN and EUINs, and every order carries the EUIN and partner reference.
  • Onboarding is composable. Nothing forces an FP-branded screen into our investor's path. We capture, you carry the registration onward. We plan to use our own identity vendor for capture.
  • Sandbox has simulation. KYC, order, payment, mandate, and transaction simulation all exist. That is better than we expected and it shapes how we plan to test.
  • Money never rests with you or us. Collection runs through the payment gateway to the AMC; redemptions settle from the AMC to the investor. You facilitate and never hold. This is the single most important thing we believe, and question 1.1 exists to get it in writing.
