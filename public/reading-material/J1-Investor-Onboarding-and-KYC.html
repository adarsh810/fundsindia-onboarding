What this document is

The governing view of onboarding a new investor to a transactable identity. It navigates the three layers the Investor reference defines, the KYC rail a record lives on, the modality used to verify, and the status that modality earns, and it draws the persona branches that change the path. The happy path ends at a Validated record, the only status that lets a new investor transact with a new fund house. It draws on Investor for the machinery and the personas, and on Rails for the identity counterparties.

What it is not

Not a reference and not a PRD. It does not restate the persona definitions, the tax overlays, or the status taxonomy, it references them. It is the identity segment of J7 drawn in depth, and it stops at a transactable identity: the lifecycle events that come after, periodic re-KYC, attaining majority, and residency change, belong to J2. V1 scope is the natural personas only: Resident Individual, NRI, OCI, and Minor, with Joint drawn as a holding-mode overlay rather than a persona. The entity personas, HUF and the non-individual entities, are an explicit, recorded deferral to the Private Wealth phase, since even HUF, the most retail-leaning of them, is a private-wealth concern rather than mass-affluent. This is a decision-surfacing pass.

1.The spine

A new resident investor moves from intent to a transactable identity through capture, verification, and status resolution. The resident individual is the baseline; every other persona is a branch off it. The journey ends not when documents are submitted but when the KRA resolves the record to Validated, because that status, and not a completed form, is what permits the first investment with a new fund house.

flowchart TD
  A([New investor intent]) --> B[Capture identity, bank proof, FATCA self-certification]
  B --> C{PAN-Aadhaar operative}
  C -->|inoperative| X1([Blocked, remediate the link first])
  C -->|operative| D[Verify via the chosen KYC modality]
  D --> E{KRA status resolved}
  E -->|Validated| F([Transactable identity])
  E -->|Registered| G[Drive to Validated via Aadhaar validation]
  G --> E
  E -->|On-Hold or Rejected| X2([Remediate the defect and re-resolve])

Two parts of this spine are where the design earns its keep, and both are drawn out below. The verify step hides a modality choice that decides whether the record can ever reach Validated, covered in Section 2. And the persona determines which capture and verification path applies, which is the primary branch. The PAN-Aadhaar operative check sits before verification deliberately: an inoperative PAN drops the record to a blocked state, so it is a pre-condition, not a post-submission surprise.

2.Decision points

Two genuine forks. The persona reroutes the path and is drawn. The modality changes whether the path can reach a transactable status, and is tabled because the choice is a selection among methods rather than a rerouting of steps.

The persona branch

For V1 the personas are the four natural ones. Each branch changes the documents, the accounts, and the verification path enough to be a genuine reroute, not just an added check.

flowchart TD
  CAP[Captured new investor] --> P{Persona}
  P -->|Resident Individual| R[Baseline, Aadhaar full KYC, self-operated]
  P -->|NRI or OCI| N[Overseas docs, NRE NRO FCNR accounts, FATCA, repatriability
Validated required, US and Canada gated by the AMC]
  P -->|Minor| M[Guardian-operated, guardian KYC, birth proof
attaining-majority freeze ahead, owned by J2]
  R --> T([Transactable identity])
  N --> T
  M --> T

Resident Individual is the baseline, self-operated, Aadhaar-validated. NRI and OCI travel together because OCI is treated on par under FEMA: both add overseas address proof, the NRE, NRO, or FCNR account that fixes repatriability, and the FATCA and CRS exposure that gates US and Canada residents. Minor holds in their own name but is operated entirely by a guardian, needs the guardian's own KYC and a relationship proof, and carries an attaining-majority freeze that J2 owns. Joint is an overlay, not a branch: it is a mode applied over any natural persona, with each holder KYC'd individually and the operation mode feeding the authority engine, so it is bulleted in Section 4 rather than drawn here. The entity personas are out of V1 entirely, and the Foreign National resident persona is a narrow natural persona surfaced as a scope question in Section 6 rather than drawn as a governing branch on this pass.

The modality menu

The modality decides the status the record can reach, and for this segment that is the whole game. The verified figures below are current to mid-2026.

Red flag: only Validated clears a new fund house, and for non-residents that bar just rose

A new investor at a new fund house needs Validated, because Registered permits transacting only where a relationship already exists. So the resident path must use an Aadhaar-based full-KYC modality, not a non-Aadhaar OVD that settles at Registered. For NRI and OCI investors this is now sharper: the relaxation that let them transact across fund houses on Registered status ran to 30 April 2026 and has lapsed, with no successor rule confirmed. A non-resident opening a new relationship must therefore reach Validated, which for capital-market products requires Aadhaar linkage even though NRIs are otherwise exempt from Aadhaar. The onboarding cannot assume a foreign-document path will transact.

3.Failure taxonomy

Onboarding breaks at a stage and, on the branches, in persona-specific ways. None of these moves money, because no folio or order exists yet, so every failure here is a remediation rather than an unwind. That is what makes the front door the cheapest place to catch a problem.

CaptureIncomplete or invalid documents, or a non-OVD document, since the 2024 tightening removed utility bills and bank statements as standalone OVDs. A missing or inoperative PAN blocks before verification.Re-capture on a valid OVD, or remediate the PAN-Aadhaar link, then proceed.
Status resolutionThe record resolves to On-Hold on an unverified mobile or email or a non-OVD document, or Rejected on mismatched documents.Fix the specific defect and re-resolve. On-Hold blocks new investment entirely.
Insufficient statusThe record reaches Registered, not a failure but not enough for a new fund house.Drive to Validated through Aadhaar validation before any first order can route.

Minor branchGuardian KYC incomplete, or the guardian-minor relationship proof missing.Complete the guardian's own full KYC and the relationship evidence; the minor's record cannot stand alone.

4.Special cases

Modes, shortcuts, and narrow conditions that change a value or add a check without rerouting the path. Listed, never drawn.

  • Existing-record fetch. A CKYC or KRA fetch that returns Validated collapses the verify step to a fetch-and-confirm. If it returns Registered, an incremental Aadhaar-validation step is added. A shortcut or an added check, not a new path.
  • The Joint overlay. Joint holding is a mode applied over a natural persona, in one of four operation modes, Jointly, Either or Survivor, Anyone or Survivor, or Former or Survivor. Each holder is KYC'd individually, and OTP-only eKYC is single-holder only so a joint holding forces a full-KYC modality. The mode feeds the authority engine but does not reroute onboarding.
  • PoA-operated non-resident. An NRI or OCI often operates through a resident power-of-attorney holder acting within registered limits. An authority overlay captured at onboarding, not a separate path. The PoA holder cannot later make a nomination, but nomination is a folio concern owned by J4.
  • Periodic re-KYC classification. KYC is not a one-time event; a risk-based re-KYC schedule, broadly two, eight, or ten years by risk class, must be set. J1 sets the initial risk classification; J2 owns the re-KYC events themselves.
  • Foreign National resident in India. A narrow natural persona, an expatriate who is a FEMA and tax resident of India. Eligibility is condition-laden and the population is small for a mass-affluent book, so it is surfaced as a scope question rather than drawn as a governing branch on this pass.

5.Entities and states exercised

The references J1 draws on, the handoffs it makes to J2 and to the folio journeys, and the one seam it shares with J7. Per the precedence rule, J1 invents no state; it references the ones the Investor reference owns.

ReferenceWhat J1 exercises
InvestorThe KYC machinery across all three layers, rail, modality, and the Validated, Registered, On-Hold, Rejected status regime; the natural-person personas and their defining attributes; operating authority for Self, Guardian, and PoA; the Joint holding-mode overlay and the repatriability tag for non-residents; and the persona lifecycle, of which the Minor-to-Major and residency-change transitions are handed to J2.
RailsThe identity rail: the KRAs and the central CKYC registry, the Aadhaar and PAN sources, and the identity middleware that wraps them, named by function. The cross-border UPI caveat on the non-resident branch.
FolioLight, by reference only: the four Joint operation modes are defined at the holding level in Folio, and the repatriability tag is set when the folio is funded in J3. J1 captures the persona and the intended mode; J3 creates the container.

Key principle: J1 ends where J7 and J3 begin, and where J2 takes over

J1 stops at a transactable, Validated identity, the exact seam J7 picks up to create the folio and place the first order. That handoff is the transactable-identity state both journeys name, confirmed here from the identity side, with no new state invented. Everything that happens to an identity after it is transactable, periodic re-KYC, attaining majority, and residency change, is a persona-lifecycle transition the Investor reference owns and J2 draws. J1 sets the initial conditions; J2 runs the lifecycle.
