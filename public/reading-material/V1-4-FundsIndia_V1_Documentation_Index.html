The PRD and spec map for the FundsIndia V1 build

Companion to the V1 Build Strategy (v2.1) and the 3-Week Lock-List · 7 PRDs, 5 specs, one shared foundation · reconciled against Build Strategy v2.1

This is the product-level decomposition of V1: which documents exist, who owns each, and what gets written when. It maps to the architecture, not to a flat feature list.

The substrate gate is the organising seam. Above it sits PRD-led work: user-facing capabilities, product-owned, each with a success metric. Below it sits spec-led work: contracts and invariants, architect and staff-eng owned, with no user stories. The Build Strategy and the Lock-List are the umbrella, so there is no separate master PRD.

Regulatory frame. Mass-affluent V1 is a distributor proposition under the MFD licence, not an RIA one. Allocation guidance is MFD-incidental, not advice-for-a-fee, and no advisory fee is charged. The MFD-not-RIA boundary is a hard compliance constraint that must be written into the recommendation-surface and Catalog PRDs (4 and 6, and the Catalog scope in 3), not just assumed. The ledger reflects only the consequence: no advisory-fee posting.

5. Transactions & MandatesPRDLumpsum, SIP, redemption, switch, STP and SWP, the mandate lifecycle, and the full money-movement UX. The vertical owner of the riskiest flow, leaning on the integration and scaffold specs for detail. Mutual-fund transactions only in V1; FDs are deferred to V1.5.
6. Portfolio & RecommendationPRDThree distinct surfaces. Portfolio dissection: deterministic decomposition only (holdings, value, XIRR, overlap, sectoral, concentration, cap split, benchmark with disclaimer), which states and never judges. Goal allocation guidance: research-driven, cohortised guidance with genuine alternatives and a what-if calculator, MFD-incidental and Compliance-gated, the bridge from goal to instrument. Financial picture (J13): the held-away after-cost view and an indicative, clearly-disclaimed tax view. Held-away holdings come through three reads in V1: the MF Central pull, the CAS OTP pull, and the AA rail (the firm is an FIU). Held-away equity surfaces read-only through CAS; no direct equity transactions. The retired "ideal portfolio recommendation" framing is replaced by the dissection-plus-guidance split. The MFD-not-RIA constraint governs every surface here, and the three highest-risk surfaces (FI Strategy back-test, the 25-year projection, the switch nudge) carry suitability and mis-selling governance from day one.
7. Referral & GrowthPRDReferral on the relationship graph, MarTech and acquisition hooks, and the clickstream taxonomy (what to track). Boundary: the clickstream data plane itself is infrastructure, in Spec 1.

Third-Party Integration ContractsSpecOne port each: FP MF, FP KYC, the payment gateway (Razorpay, FP-native), CAS and MF Central for the held-away pull, and the Account Aggregator adapter (single-AA in V1, the firm as FIU, written to a vendor-neutral interface). DSequencing · what to write when

The sequence follows the Build Strategy's five build stages and the two gates. The ramp window is spec-led and research-led, not PRD-led; the heavy PRD work lands once the substrate is proven.

EBoundaries to hold

Seams agreed during decomposition, recorded so two documents do not both claim the same thing. Recommendation and guidance logic lives in PRD 6; Discovery (PRD 4) only surfaces it; the goal contract it plugs into is in PRD 2. The Commercial Relationship and user-advisor mappings are defined in Spec 1; their establishment lifecycle is in PRD 1. The clickstream taxonomy (what to track) is product, in PRD 7; the clickstream data plane is infrastructure, in Spec 1. The consent rights-centre UX is in PRD 1; the consent ledger is in Spec 5. Held-away holdings come through three reads in V1, the MF Central and CAS OTP pulls (certification-free) and the AA rail (the firm as FIU, Sahamati certification in progress), with the experience in PRD 6 and the integrations in Spec 4. The multi-AA gateway is a V1.5 upgrade behind the same AA port, kept loosely coupled so it can be added without gutting either.
Open, pending closure. Recommendation-surface policing: whether the three recommendation-shaped surfaces (allocation guidance in PRD 6, dissection tagging in PRD 6, discovery pills in PRD 4) police the MFD dissection-not-prescription boundary independently or route through one shared enforcement point. PRDs 2, 4, and 6 are kept separate for now; the policing decision does not change that split, but once landed it may add a one-line enforcement-point note to PRD 4 and PRD 6. Tracked as the architecture-view open decision, not yet closed.

Ownership note: the PRD-versus-spec label is a deliberate split of accountability. PRDs are product-owned and answer what and why for a user-facing capability with a success metric. Specs are architect or staff-eng owned and answer how, in contracts and invariants. Keeping the labels distinct keeps both the owner and the definition of done unambiguous for each document.
