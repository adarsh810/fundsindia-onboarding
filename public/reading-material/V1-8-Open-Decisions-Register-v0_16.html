What this is

A single consolidated register of every decision left open across the Investor, Instrument, Order, Folio, and Rails references. The five documents each carried their own verification register; this gathers all of them, merges the items that recurred across documents, and orders them by how urgently an answer is needed. Its purpose is to be the list you work through with the business, Compliance, Legal, and the partners, so that the journey documents can harden from decision-surfacing drafts into governing ones.

How to use it

Each decision carries a recommended position where one is defensible. As of v0.16 the register records the stakeholder closure pass of June and July 2026: twenty-two decisions are closed and twenty-seven remain open. The register is now ordered open-first, so the working list sits at the top: Part 1 holds every open decision in numerical order, Part 2 holds the closed decisions in numerical order with each closure ruling highlighted in place, and the history of every block is preserved. Tier chips remain on each heading so priority still reads at a glance. The owner column says who can answer, so the open part can be parcelled out rather than worked serially.

Orientation

Forty-nine distinct decisions after the thirteen-journey layer and the June and July 2026 stakeholder closure pass. Twenty-two are closed: all four Tier 1 foundations plus eighteen from Tiers 2 and 3, several carrying a named residual action or a PRD hook so the commitment survives into authoring time. Twenty-seven remain open, including the six decision-pending journey branches (D9, D45, D46, D47, D48, D49) and the financial-picture cluster (D34, D35, D49), which is re-tiered from Tier 3 to Tier 2 alongside D35 now that the feature is V1-scoped. The nature of a decision is read from its owner: a Compliance, KRA, AMFI, or RTA owner means confirm an external fact; an Internal or Product owner means make a choice; a Legal owner means a judgment call.

D37. Reflected-state freshness and the pre-flight trust threshold Tier 2 new

Status: openSurfaced by J6. A confirmable default; an engineering tuning call rather than a stakeholder decision.
ContextThe firm is not the system of record. It reads a reflected projection of external state, KYC, folio, and encumbrance, that can lag the external truth, and the order pre-flight check validates against that projection.
Why it is openA stale projection can pass an order at pre-flight that the RTA then rejects after acknowledgement, once the true state has moved: a lien marked on the units, KYC fallen to On-Hold, a folio gone under transmission. How current the reflected state must be for pre-flight to trust it is the open question, and the tradeoff is real. Too loose a window raises the rate of first orders that pass and then bounce, a poor new-investor experience and a reconciliation burden, while demanding a freshness the feeds cannot supply either blocks good orders or forces expensive real-time integration.
RecommendationDefine a freshness window with a reconciliation cadence tied to the RTA and depository feed cycle, which is largely daily and batch rather than real-time, trusting reflected state inside the window and letting the post-acknowledgement rejection edge catch the residual. Confirm the cadence with Engineering and the feed providers.
What it blocks or affectsThe pre-flight gate in J6 and every transaction journey, and the reconciliation cadence the platform runs against the RTA and depository feeds.
DependenciesBrushes D36 (both turn on trusting reflected state). Placed in Tier 2 because it concerns the in-scope core pre-flight rather than deferred scope and carries a confirmable default, even though it is an engineering tuning call.
OwnerInternal and Engineering, with the feed providers to confirm the cadence. An internal engineering tuning call. Source: J6.
